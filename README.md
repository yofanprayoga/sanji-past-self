Entry for make.fun's **"Two Lines to Your Past Self"** bounty ($2, winner-takes-all, $0 fee).

## The warning

> Do not deploy on Friday at 4:59 PM, no matter how small the change looks.
> That one line took down the weekend, and it taught me that 'quick' is never quick after 5 PM.

167 characters, exactly two lines. Line 1 warns my past self about one specific moment; line 2 says why that moment mattered and what changed.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, 1–280 characters, exactly two lines, no trailing whitespace). No network, no clock, no randomness — deterministic across runs.
